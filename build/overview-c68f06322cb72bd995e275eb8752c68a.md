# Curricular Materials for First-Year Engineering

## Overview

As a curriculum developer for **First-Year Engineering (FYE)** at Purdue University, I design the coursework and the platforms it runs on. The work sits between instructional design and software engineering: evidence-based course design on one side, version control and automated builds on the other.


## Teaching Philosophy

The materials I design are built to:

- Connect theoretical concepts to real-world applications
- Encourage critical thinking and problem-solving
- Support diverse learning styles through multimodal content
- Promote active learning through hands-on activities
- Foster student confidence and self-efficacy in STEM
